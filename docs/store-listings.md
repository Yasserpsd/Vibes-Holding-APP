المحايد يجد في النادي المكان الذي يتعرّف فيه على عالم الأعمال ويبني علاقاته قبل أن يختار طريقه. ورائد الأعمال يجد من يؤمن بفكرته ويشاركه بناءها. والمستثمر يلتقي بالفرص مبكراً ويتعرّف على أصحابها عن قرب.

وداخل التطبيق تجد العديد من فرص الشراكة، تتصفّحها وتتواصل مع أصحابها مباشرة.

Investors Club: a business club based in Riyadh, bringing together three groups under one roof: Neutrals, Entrepreneurs and Investors.

In business, relationships matter most. A deal starts with an introduction, a partnership starts with a meeting, and opportunities usually go to those who are close to them. The club was built on this belief.

Neutrals find a place to discover the world of business and build their network before choosing their path. Entrepreneurs find people who believe in their idea and want to help build it. Investors meet opportunities early and get to know the people behind them up close.

Inside the app, you will find many partnership opportunities to browse, with direct contact with their owners.

Download the app, choose your category, and start your membership today.

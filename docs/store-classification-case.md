## Letter (Apple version)

Subject: Classification inquiry — annual club membership that primarily covers in-person services (App: Investors Club, Apple ID 6825963092)

Dear App Review team,

We operate Investors Club, a business club with a physical headquarters in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia (app: "Investors Club", Apple ID 6825963092, published by Virtual Community Investment Company / vcmem.com, part of the Vibes Ventures ecosystem). We currently sell our annual membership in the app exclusively through In-App Purchase, in full compliance with guideline 3.1.1, and we intend to remain fully compliant whatever the outcome of this inquiry.

We are writing to ask for your guidance on the correct classification of this membership, because its substance is overwhelmingly a real-world service consumed outside the app:

- The club runs a physical headquarters in Riyadh — meeting rooms, private offices, a recording studio and an 80-seat auditorium — with an annual lease above SAR 400,000. Active members book and use these facilities at no extra charge.
- Throughout the year the club holds in-person meetups, evening gatherings and workshops. The club pays for hotel venues, catering at every gathering, and external facilitators who deliver the workshops. Admission to all of these events is included in the membership at no extra charge.
- The membership fee (SAR 1,900 per year) is, in economic substance, the price of this physical hospitality: venue costs, catering, facilitator fees and facility access make up the overwhelming majority of what the fee funds.
- The app's digital features are companions to the physical experience: booking HQ rooms, viewing the events agenda, contacting the club's administration, and browsing partnership opportunities whose purpose is to lead to in-person meetings.

Guideline 3.1.3(e) provides that apps may use purchase methods other than in-app purchase for goods and services that will be consumed outside of the app. Given the facts above, we would like to ask:

1. Does our annual membership qualify, in whole or in part, as a service consumed outside of the app under 3.1.3(e)?
2. If not as currently structured, is there a structure you would consider compliant — for example, a membership covering only the physical services (facility access, meetups, workshops, hospitality) sold outside of IAP, with the app's digital features provided to members at no charge?

We can provide supporting documentation: the HQ lease, invoices for catering, hotel venues and workshop facilitators, and our events calendar.

We appreciate the App Store and simply want to classify this correctly. Thank you for your time and guidance.

Kind regards,

## Letter (Google version — adapt before sending)

Subject: Payments policy scope question — club membership that primarily covers in-person services (com.vcmem.investorsclub)

Same facts as above, with the ask adapted to Play's Payments policy: Google Play's billing requirement applies to digital goods and services, while physical services must NOT use Play Billing. Ask: (1) whether the annual membership, whose economic substance is physical hospitality and event access, is classified as a physical service for which an alternative payment flow (our PCI-compliant local processor, Moyasar) is required or permitted in-app; (2) if the current hybrid is treated as digital, whether a membership restructured to cover only the physical services would be classified as physical. Note that subscriptions currently use Play Billing in full compliance.

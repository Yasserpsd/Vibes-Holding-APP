Many of you have asked why we were absent from some of the major meetups and exhibitions over the past period, and we would like to give you the full picture.

Why were we not there?
Our absence was not a retreat from the scene, nor a dismissal of how important these gatherings are — it was a deliberate decision.
We were in the final stages of building the Vibes Ventures ecosystem, putting the finishing touches on more than 3 years of work.
We were also completing the local and international licences, permits and paperwork needed to launch the Investors Club® app.

We chose to build the ecosystem first… before going out to present it.
Our next appearance, in 2027, will be completely different:
venues that match the scale of the ecosystem, and a presence that reflects the value of what we offer — not mere attendance for the record or for the photos.

The reason is simple:
we do not want to offer people a single day of opportunities, but an ecosystem that stays with them for a whole year.

What really happens at the big meetups?
At gatherings like Biban and LEAP, hundreds of thousands of visitors come looking for knowledge, relationships, opportunities, investment and partnerships.
Yet by their very nature, the direct benefit is concentrated in less than 1% of the attendees: the exhibiting companies' owners, their partners, and the participating organizations.

The far larger segment — 99% of the attendees — are the people we call "neutrals":
visitors who have not yet chosen their direction. Will they become investors? Entrepreneurs? Partners in projects?
Their experience usually ends with a passing visit of two or three days; the meetup closes, and everyone goes home.

This is exactly where Investors Club® comes in
Your relationship with us is not tied to an event held once or twice a year.
We want you as an active member of an ecosystem that works with you all year round, through a distinctive app where you can:
• browse and follow projects and discover opportunities;
• attend workshops, meetups, networking gatherings and private evenings;
• build relationships with entrepreneurs, investors and fellow club members —
in person at the club's headquarters in the heart of Riyadh, or remotely for members elsewhere.

In other words:
an exhibition or a meetup brings people together in one place for a day or two…
As a club member, the whole ecosystem stays with you, at your fingertips, for 360 days through the app.
That is the essence of what we mean by the sharing economy:
the neutral is not merely a recipient, the entrepreneur is not merely a project owner, and the investor is not merely a source of capital —
everyone is part of a living ecosystem, full of activity, delivering real value across an entire year.

Our focus in the next phase: the neutrals
They represent a large part of the community, and many of them go to meetups looking for an opportunity without yet knowing where they will stand.
We want to give them something bigger than a passing chance — a full year of possibilities:
✅ A year of projects.
✅ A year of relationships.
✅ A year of workshops.
✅ A year of meetups.
✅ A year of evenings.
✅ A year of networking.
✅ And a full year to discover where they truly belong.

We have been working quietly behind the scenes to build the platform that will take us to the market differently.
God willing, in 2027 the question will no longer be: "Did Investors Club attend?"
It will be: how big is the Investors Club booth?
